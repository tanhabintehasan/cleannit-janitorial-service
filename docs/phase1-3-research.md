1. Every top-5 competitor runs FAQ schema and "People Also Ask"-style FAQ blocks → mandatory per page.

4. Niche services (balcony cleaning, bird netting) have weak SERPs → fastest ranking wins.

# PHASE 3 — CONTENT BRIEFS (per template)

Benchmark from SERP median: service pages ~1,000–1,400 words; location pages ~800–1,100; blog cost guides ~1,500–2,000.

## Service page brief (×7)
- Target keyword: "[service] toronto"; supporting: semantic terms from §3
- Word count: 1,000–1,400 (SERP median)
- Structure: H1 (keyword + CTA) → intro 150–200w (keyword + Toronto in first 100w) → what's included (H2/H3) → benefits → common problems → 5-step process → why choose us (licence #736686338, insured, founder-led) → gallery 3–6 captioned images → 5–7 FAQs (PAA-matched, incl. pricing) → related services → CTA (tel: + quote form)
- FAQ count: 6 (match PAA density of MCA/ServiceMaster pages)
- Media: hero + 3 gallery images, WebP, alt < 125 chars
- Internal links: 3–5 (related services, nearest locations, cost-guide blog)
- External authority links: 1–2 (e.g., City of Toronto bylaws, WSIB, Health Canada)
- E-E-A-T: licence number, founder name + photo, real fleet photo, "last updated" date
