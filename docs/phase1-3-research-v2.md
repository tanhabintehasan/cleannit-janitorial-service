## Why they rank — strategy synthesis
1. **Word count depth wins competitive terms**: commercial/location clusters rank at 2,400–2,900 words with dense H2/H3 structure (12–16 H2s). Our pages must meet ~1,500–2,500 words on money pages.
2. **Hero lead forms are universal** on the strongest pages (MCA, Skyrex, Hawkeye, DT Cleaning) → schedule form in every hero, right column.
3. **FAQ schema + visible reviews** correlate with rankings (Skyrex, Balcony Cleaners) → FAQPage schema everywhere (done) + visible 139-review aggregate + Review schema.
4. **Two big names rank with near-zero crawlable content** (JAN-PRO, Shine Windows JS shells) — they win on brand/backlinks only → beatable on content depth + CWV.
5. **Pricing transparency** (dtcleaning, balconycleaners "from $165") earns snippets and conversions → every service page gets a pricing-guidance block with researched GTA ranges.
6. **E-E-A-T signals**: licence number, founding year, review count, founder identity, real photos — all on-page and in schema (foundingDate 2020, aggregateRating 139).
